# ParaVario: how to use it

For a ParaVario with a GPS module. Settings are explained in [Feature_Reference.md](Feature_Reference.md).

## 1. Before you fly
1. Switch on at the launch and wait for **"Ready"**, then **"GPS fix"**.
2. Take off. You hear **"Take off"**. Heights are measured from where you took off.

There are no beeps on the ground. They start after takeoff.

## 2. Push button

| Press | Does |
|---|---|
| **1** | Status: height, landing zone and wind |
| **2** | Landing approach on or off |
| **Hold** | Emergency glide on or off. Hold until the vario speaks. |
| **3** | Beeps muted or on |

## 3. In flight
- **"250 feet"** as you pass set heights, and your height every minute.
- **"Flight time …"** every 5 minutes.
- **"Battery low"**: you can switch the vario off to save the battery. Switch it on again a couple of minutes before you need it.
- **"No GPS"**: height and beeps carry on.

## 4. Landing approach
Press twice and follow the calls. At the crosswind call, turn either way. Press twice again to cancel.

## 5. Emergency glide
Hold the button and follow the calls. Hold again to cancel.

## 6. Safe area
If you have set a safe area, the vario says **"You have left the safe area"** when you go outside it, and **"Back in the safe area"** when you return. Before you reach a restricted area you hear **"Restricted area, 400 metres. Turn right to 2 o'clock."**

## 7. After landing
On the **Logs & system** tab, each flight has **Replay**, **Download** and **Delete**. Download each day's flights: the oldest are deleted when space runs out.

## 8. Phone page
1. Join the Wi-Fi **ParaVario-XXXX**. The page opens by itself, or go to **http://192.168.4.1**.
2. **Volume** tile on the Flight tab: **+** and **−** change the beep and voice volume.
3. The Flight tab buttons do the same as the push button. The marks show the presses: **[.]** 1, **[..]** 2, **[...]** 3, **[—]** hold.

**iPhone:** in Safari tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**. **Android:** tap **Full screen**.

## 9. Settings

| I want to | Go to |
|---|---|
| Use metres, km/h, mph | Settings → Units |
| Change the spoken heights | Settings → Height calls → Boundaries (heights separated by commas or spaces) |
| Land in another field | Settings → Landing zone → stand in the field and tap **Use current position** |
| Land where I took off again | Settings → Landing zone → *Takeoff point* |
| Have beeps on the ground | Settings → Vario → Mute until flight (off) |
| Choose the circuit side | Settings → Landing approach → Circuit direction |
| Stay inside my flying area | Map tab → New area, tap the map for each corner, Save area |

## 10. FlyGaggle
Add a Bluetooth vario and pick **ParaVario-XXXX**.

## 11. If something is wrong

| Problem | Do this |
|---|---|
| No beeps in flight | The Vario tile says *muted*: press 3 times. |
| Cannot find the Wi-Fi | Look for ParaVario- followed by four characters. |
| The vario thinks you are flying | **Logs & system** → **Reset flight state**. |
| Anything else | Switch off and on. Settings are kept. |

## 12. Factory reset
On the ground. Press the button 5 times and wait for the prompt, then press 5 times and hold a sixth press for 2 seconds. The vario restarts with every setting back to its default.
